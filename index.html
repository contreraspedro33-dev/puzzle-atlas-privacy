Puzzle Atlas · Android app

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · App package: com.puzzleatlas.game

In short: Puzzle Atlas has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress stays on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

Puzzle Atlas is a puzzle game for Android published by Pedro Contreras ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If you have questions, contact us at contreraspedro33@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, lives, hints used and settings such as sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store it on any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and how you interact with ads
  • Diagnostic information, such as performance and crash data related to ads

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact menu varies by device). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

Buying Premium removes all ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The optional Premium upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We never see or store your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Premium has been purchased on your Google account, so it can unlock Premium and restore it if you reinstall.

If you contact us or rate the app

If you use Send feedback in Settings, your phone opens your own email app with a message to us. It includes the app version, your phone model and Android version so we can help with problems. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply and improve the game. Rating and sharing use Google Play and your phone's share menu; the app does not receive your review or your contacts.

Children

Puzzle Atlas is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
